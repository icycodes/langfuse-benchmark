Its prompt management product supports an advanced feature called **message placeholders** that allow you to insert a list of chat messages at specific positions within a chat prompt. The placeholders are resolved at runtime via the `compile()` method, so you can dynamically inject conversation history or few-shot examples while keeping the rest of the prompt versioned in Langfuse.
